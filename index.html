Dear Gentleman, 🥺

I know, I know… mai kuch dino se kuch zyada hi alag behave kar rahi hoon. Bematlab gussa karti hoon, chidchidi ho gayi hoon aur aapko taunts bhi maarti rehti hoon.

But believe me, mai aisi bilkul nahi hoon, Mr. Gentleman.

Pehle aapka time, attention aur pyaar mil jaata tha. Lekin ab kuch dino se aap busy hain aur mujhe utna time nahi de paa rahe. Mujhe samajhna chahiye ki aapki bhi apni responsibilities hain, apni padhai aur kaam hain. Lekin pata nahi kyun, mai samajhne ke bajaye pagal ki tarah aap par hi gussa karne lagti hoon.

You are right, mai sach mein buddhu hoon. 🤦🏻‍♀️

Mujhe apne behaviour ka regret ho raha hai. Mujhe aapse aise baat nahi karni chahiye thi, especially jab aap meri life mein itne special hain.

Mai aapse naraz ho sakti hoon, aapko miss kar sakti hoon, aapke time ki expectation bhi rakh sakti hoon… but iska matlab ye toh nahi ki apni saari frustration aap par nikaal doon.

I am sorry, Gentleman.

Bas itna chahti hoon ki aap meri in kuch dino wali chidchidhi version ko hi meri poori personality mat samajh lijiyega.

Aapki wohi purani Hoor-e-Pareshan abhi bhi yahin hai… thodi si pagal, thodi si childish, bahut si emotional aur aapko tang karne mein expert. 🤭❤️
